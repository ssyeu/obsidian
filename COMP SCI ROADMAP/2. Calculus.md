[[math]]
Limits: where a function converges to, where it goes to when its undefined, ex: the function is approaching a certain value. we can find limits of functions that are undefined by factoring them and simplifying and using the remaining parts to find what value it approaches 

Derivatives: functions that find the slope of a function at a value.

Integrals: opposite of derivatives, finding the area under a curve. measures accumulation. Integrals are anti derivatives, doing one on the other undoes what it did.
