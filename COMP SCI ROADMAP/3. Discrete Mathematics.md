proposition: a declarative sentence that is either true or false, but not both. True or false questions. propositions that cannot be expressed in terms of simpler propositions are called atomic propositions. New propositions are called compound propositions formed from existing ones using logical operators. You can negate a proposition or mean the opposite of it with some symbols which vary depending on who you talk to. If the proposition p is True the negation of p is False. conjoining 2 propositions means that the resulting compound proposition is only true if both of them are true, and false otherwise. The disjunction of 2 propositions is that the compound proposition is false when both are false, and true otherwise. exclusive is its true when only one of either is true and false otherwise. A conditional statement is a proposition if p, then q. It is false when p is true and q is false, and true otherwise. weird one. It asserts that q is true on the condition that p holds
