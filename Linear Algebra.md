1. essence of linear algebra is in vectors. Vectors are arrows in space, with a length and direction. They can be two or three dimensional. Vectors can also be a list of numbered pairs of 2 or more. The order in the groups matter. The mathematician says the vector can be anything as long as you can add or multiply them. Adding a vector is like adding x1 and x2 and then y1 and y2, or moving the base of the second vector to the tip of the first vector, the base of the first vector to the tip of the second vector is the sum of both vectors. Multiplying a vector by a constant is called scaling it, the number multiplying by them is called the scalar. You just stretch or squish the vector by the scalar, if the scalar is negative, you flip the vector across its base and then stretch or squish it.
