he talks about the Bahnzhaf power index which talks about how power is distributed in a situation where 51% of votes or shares is needed. For example, 3 people with 47%, 45%, and 7% of the shares might seem to be vastly different in their power, but they all have equal power. That is because any 2 of them have to vote the same way to create the 51% needed to win a vote, the last person even if they have far less of a percentage just needs one of the other two to win a vote just like the person with 47%. Another situation would be 4 people wtih 26%, 24%, 23%, and 22% of shares. The last person wit 22% might seem just as equal
