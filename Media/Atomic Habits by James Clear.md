This book was solidly okay, it had some decent advice and some advice that wasn't that decent. I think it was a book written for the ordinary person with little struggle in their lives other than wanting to make some better habits. It seems a little out of touch with the problems of regular life and seems to not even understand that some of the things he recommends might be difficult for someone, it's very strange. He does give some good advice i agree with though, start small, do things consistently, always do it, try to be the person you want to be, not just setting a goal you will ignore, don't make things too hard. Like it seems all good and well and then he will just be like oh if smokers knew that smoking didn't give them anything good they would stop smoking. LIKE WHAT, that doesn't make any sense? Is he implying that most smokers just think that smoking is good for them in some way? It's strange, it seems very one view, like the authors point of view and he doesn't think how things can be different for different people. Hopefully i took the good I needed out of the book I am trying to implement one main thing which is to take actions i think the person i want to be will. To try and be that person without having any goal in particular out of mind, whenever i come to decision moments I think about what the person I want to become will do.
