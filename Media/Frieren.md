This show does its job very well of being slice of life while still having good aura moments, i think it does it very well. We learn so much about the characters in some episodes and it makes us care a lot more about them which makes the aura farming hit so much more. The time where we learn Frieren was called Freiren the Slayer after this build up of her being this cute little elf makes it hit so much harder and where we learn about her mana look so much cooler than if it was just shown out of the box without anything. The [[buildup]] is what made us care about the character, not just the aura farming moments

I think the episode where himmel is shown not to pull the sword out of the stone was really good too. It shows how we romanticize our heroes and they become more than what they are. Stories of their failures are forgotten and their best aspects are glowed up. They become the ideal hero and over time their true self becomes lost.

The episode about Stark was good, it made his character more complex and i like how it shows our heroes as not perfect. He sucked at being a warrior as a kid and he grew from that, it was sad that no one appreciated him but his brother and it was his brother that told him to run from the village and he lives with that guilt that he did even though he was told to.
