You'll Never Walk Alone:
This chapter was written during COVID I think, John talks about something going horribly wrong and the end of the chapter mentioned paramedics in 2020 but it could be unrelated. It talks about a song from a play called you'll never walk alone and how it doesn't mean that you won't have sorrows but you'll walk through them. He says a core part of humanity is that they keep walking through it all. I think that's interesting because people give up every day. It seems oddly positive to me but I think it resonates with most people.
