You'll Never Walk Alone:
This chapter was written during COVID I think, John talks about something going horribly wrong and the end of the chapter mentioned paramedics in 2020 but it could be unrelated. It talks about a song from a play called you'll never walk alone and how it doesn't mean that you won't have sorrows but you'll walk through them. It seems oddly positive to me but I think it resonates with most people.

The sun will make Earth uninhabitable in a billion years and boil its oceans, in 4 billion it will melt the crust and in 7 or 8 it will consume the Earth. John talks about the anxiety that this knowledge gave him and how he always thought humanity was on its lost quarter. Humanity was a young species, only living 250,000 years. A mere 2 minutes if life's history was a year long. People always thought they were in the end times and John thought the same during the COVID pandemic. He went to his brother for comfort and all he could give him was that the species will survive this. Humanity has been an ecological disaster but it's destruction is limited compared to other extinctions.  Rather human pox than a large asteroid disease. Life will survive humans. It's worrying that humanity is powerful enough to cause disasters like global warming but not powerful enough to fix them. A transitional phase. Humans are persistent though. They hunted through persistence hunting and they survied that way too. We will find a way to make it through. John is very not individualistic, he cares about the speicies. Personally I wouldn't care about the species, i would be far more interested in myself and the people in my immediate surroundings.
