Halley's Comet:
The only periodic comet that can be seen from Earth with the naked eye. It has a period of 74 years. Edmund Halley was not the first to notice the comet but he was the first to predict when it would come next, when the comet came the year he said it was named after him. John talks about how science isn't just one guy figuring things out. It's a whole system. The reason people started figuring everything out in the universe in previous ages wasn't because they were any smarter than their ancestors. It was the dawn of the industrial revolution and most of this happened in Europe where there was so much wealth people could have the time to dedicate to scientific endeavors. With institutions such as the Royal Society that let scientists share their data with each other easier than ever before. It was also the beginning of the scientific method, a formalized way for science to be studied. Johns example for Halley's brilliance was interesting to me though. To find the area of Europe he cut a circle of known area on a map, then weighed it against a map of Europe and found that the European map was 4 times heavier, reasoning that there must be 4 times the area of the known circle. That is so brilliant and I would have never thought about weighing a map. He was within 1% of modern measurements. John talks about the time when he built a bench with his dad when he was a child in a cabin in the woods to watch Halley's Comet, how he treasured that moment and considers it one of his most important. 20 years later even he took his girlfriend to the place and the disheveled bench still sat there and still took their weight and held. Halley's comet will come 5 times closer next time than it did last. It won't be any harm but it will be a sight to see. I'm excited to see it. He measures time in Halley's and it really puts things in perspective. How much can change over the span of so few human lives. His father and him built that bench. One Halley earlier and the first Frankenstein movie was released, another and Charles Darwin was on his expedition, another and The United states didn't exist, another and Louis XIV ruled France. Time is so short, years may seem long but only 5 human life times span between us and the Taj Mahal being built
