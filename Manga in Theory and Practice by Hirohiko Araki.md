Araki talks about how he believes in the golden road of manga, and that all good manga follow this road or deliberately subvert it to create their narrative. The first chapter was mainly about the beginning of a manga, the first page, title, and cover. It's very important to have a good first impression since it was common that editors would simply pull out a manga, see its cover or first page, and reject the author without looking further. He talks about the 5 Ws and 1 H, who is doing what and when and where and why and how. He wants to share as much info about the story at the first page.
